Scope: the Steady weekly dashboard (one page). Mode: Operate. Owner checks it a few times a week on phone or laptop.
Job: answer "Am I improving?" and "Am I overtraining?" at a glance, see the week's sessions, request a Claude review.
Constraints: all IDs/behaviour/a11y kept; fonts self-hosted; no CDN; no build step.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the week as a designer's working sketch sheet, ruled boxes drawn in ink on sketch paper, not a colour-block poster or a SaaS card grid. Pinned by the owner's brief ("Conceptual Sketch") and reference posters.
OWN-WORLD: grey-warm sketch paper with grain; ink #1d1c1a and two graphite greys; one printed spot green for meaning only (verdict yes, improving rows, the "fresh" side of the gauge, today). Wobbly double-stroke ink boxes, crosshatching density for effort and activity kind, Cabin Sketch display lettering, Kalam handwritten annotations, Work Sans for numbers.
STORY: owner reads the verdict word, the gauge, then the numbers and the week; trusts it because notes say what each number compares against; presses Review to get Claude's take.
FIRST VIEWPORT: ink header strip with week name and Review button; big hand-lettered verdict circled in a scribble with an annotation arrow; hatched gauge beside it; running, weight, strength boxes below; the week row of hatched day blocks.
FORM: pinned brief (no roll; brief-pinned direction beats the roll). Signature move: effort and session kind drawn as crosshatch density inside hand-ruled day boxes. Seed key: none (pinned).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
